# Gender Reveal Project – Background Context

## Overview
This project is a small, playful website built to support a gender reveal event for friends. The goal is to create a light, engaging experience that builds anticipation over time rather than delivering everything at once.

The site will be shared with friends ahead of the reveal and updated over a period of approximately 5 weeks.

Primary objective:
- Entertain and tease guests leading up to the reveal
- Encourage repeat visits
- Keep the implementation simple and maintainable

---

**Bambi (Father)**
- Works as an internal auditor
- Enjoys sports
- Participates in endurance events (e.g. half Ironman)

**Tahnee (Mother)**
- Relaxed personality

---

## Tone and Style Direction

The experience should feel:
- Playful
- Slightly mysterious
- Teasing, but not overly complex

Avoid:
- Overengineering
- Too many features
- Anything requiring user accounts or friction

The site should feel like a running joke or shared experience rather than a formal event page.

---

## Core Idea

A simple website that evolves over time:

- Guests visit the site periodically
- New clues or content appear weekly
- Users can make predictions and compare with others
- The actual reveal replaces the site content on the final day

---

## Interaction Goals

- Let users guess the baby’s gender
- Let users see what others think
- Provide small, periodic updates ("clues")
- Build curiosity without ever fully revealing the answer early

---

## Timeline

Approximate duration: **5 weeks leading up to the reveal**

Suggested cadence:
- Weekly clue updates
- Ongoing prediction submissions
- Increasing tension toward final week

---

## Initial Ideas Considered

The following concepts were explored early on:

- Using invisible ink for physical teasing
- Building a small app or interactive experience
- Creating a website as the central hub for engagement

Final direction chosen:
- A **simple website** that hosts all interaction

---

## Website Role in the Experience

The website acts as:
- The central place for all clues and interaction
- A way to involve guests before the event
- A controlled channel for teasing the reveal

It should not try to do too much. Simplicity is a core constraint.

---

## Key Design Constraints

- Minimal pages
- Minimal backend complexity
- No authentication
- Fast to update (especially for weekly clues)

---

## Narrative Angle (Optional but Recommended)

A light narrative layer can be used to unify the experience.

Suggested direction:
- “Investigation” / “Case File” theme
- Inspired by Bambi’s auditing background

Examples:
- Referring to clues as "evidence"
- Referring to the baby as a "classified result"
- Using the dogs as "unreliable witnesses"

This should remain subtle and not block usability.

---

## Success Criteria

The project is successful if:

- Guests visit the site more than once
- People engage with predictions or clues
- The reveal feels built-up rather than sudden
- The site remains simple to manage throughout

---

## Notes for Future Development

- Prioritise clarity over cleverness
- Add features only if they directly increase engagement
- Prefer static/simple solutions where possible
